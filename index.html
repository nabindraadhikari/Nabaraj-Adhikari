Early Roots & Childhood

Born and raised in Gorkha, his father Ram Hari Adhikari and mother Him Kumari Adhikari were a teacher and a nurse. His mind and focus were driven toward engineering from an early age. He studied at Shree Bindhyavasini Secondary School. In his SLC examination, he scored 99 out of 100—a remarkable milestone that reflected his dedication and belief that nothing is impossible.

Career Highlights in Nepal

He began his professional path at Lama Construction Company Pvt. Ltd. and went on to work with leading organizations including Dywidag-Dragados, Himal Hydro and General Construction Ltd., and Mandakini Hydropower Company. His career showcases extensive experience managing key infrastructure projects across Nepal.

International Career

Expanding his professional horizon globally, he has contributed to major international engineering firms including AFRY Energy Ltd. and Pöyry Energy Ltd.
